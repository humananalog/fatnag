The Scale Privacy Policy

Controller: Human Analog Limited
App: The Scale (iOS), bundle app.thescale.ios
Contact: privacy@humananalog.ai

1. Who we are
Human Analog Limited provides The Scale. Most weighing, Health, and profile data stays on your iPhone. Optional Keel Coach may send chat and digests to our systems only after you consent.

2. What we process
On-device: scale readings, calibration, profile, meal plans, Coach memory, preferences, local notifications, Apple Health data you authorize.
Optional Keel Coach: chat text and short fitness digests over HTTPS via our Cloudflare Worker to xAI. No ads. No sale of personal data.
Apple: App Store / StoreKit billing; on-device Apple Intelligence when available.

3. Children
Adults 18+ only.

4. Your rights
Export / Erase in Settings, or email privacy@humananalog.ai (GDPR / CCPA-style requests).

5. Full text
Open Settings → Privacy & Legal inside The Scale for the complete policy, US State Privacy Notice, Terms, Medical Disclaimer, Subscriptions, and Limitation of Liability.
